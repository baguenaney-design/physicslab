## Key Equations
- $\Sigma F = ma$ Newton's second law
- $N = mg$ normal force — level surface, applied force horizontal
- $f_s \le \mu_s N$ static friction — an inequality, not an equality
- $f_{s,max} = \mu_s N$ maximum static friction, at the point of slipping
- $f_k = \mu_k N$ kinetic friction — independent of speed
- $F_{net} = F_{app} - f$ net force along the surface
- $v = u + at$ velocity under constant acceleration
- $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$ displacement under constant acceleration
